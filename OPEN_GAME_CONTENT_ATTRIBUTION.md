# Open Game Content Attribution

This project can locally sync open D&D 2024 SRD data from the community-maintained 5e-bits/5e-database dataset.

The underlying Dungeons & Dragons System Reference Document 5.2.x content is provided by Wizards of the Coast under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

Synced local datasets used by this project:
- 2024 class features
- 2024 feats
- 2024 spells
- 2024 equipment, weapons, armor, tools and packs
- 2024 magic items
- 2024 backgrounds
- 2024 poisons

The sync step copies only the open SRD datasets into generated local TypeScript files. User-owned, non-SRD book PDFs and their derived indexes stay in ignored local folders and are never part of the repository.
